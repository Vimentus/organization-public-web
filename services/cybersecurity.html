Why it matters, even for small companies

Attackers don't pick targets by size. They pick the easiest way in. One phishing email or one unpatched laptop can stop a small business for weeks, and the bill lands on you. Being small is not protection.

01

Threat Detection

SIEM Deployment & Tuning

We collect the logs from your systems in one place and turn them into alerts that point to real attacks, not noise.

  • Log sources onboarded
  • Detection use cases for your setup
  • Alert tuning to cut false alarms
  • Dashboards and a health check

EDR Deployment & Tuning

Endpoint protection on every laptop and server that spots and stops suspicious behaviour as it happens.

  • Rollout plan for all devices
  • Policy configuration and exclusions
  • Alert tuning
  • Response playbooks for your team

Network Security

Network Segmentation & Firewalls

We split your network into zones, so one infected device can't reach everything else.

  • Zone design and VLANs
  • Firewall rule review and clean-up
  • Network documentation

Secure Remote Access

Safe access to company systems from anywhere, without leaving the door open.

  • VPN or zero-trust access setup
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Device checks before access
  • Clear access policies

  • Risk-based prioritisation

Security Posture Assessment
